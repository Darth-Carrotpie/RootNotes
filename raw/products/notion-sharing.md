# Sharing & permissions settings in Notion

> Source: https://www.notion.com/en-gb/help/sharing-and-permissions
> Collected: 2026-11-02
> Published: Unknown

Teamspaces are dedicated spaces for every team within your organization or company. Each teamspace has its own members and permission levels, which can be customized by its teamspace owners.

If you’re not able to share a page with a guest, this might be because:

* Your workspace has reached its guest limit.
* Your workspace or teamspace has prevented guests from being added to the page.

Guests are people who aren’t part of your Notion workspace. This could be a contractor or a client you’re working with.
